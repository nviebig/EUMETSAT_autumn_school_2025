🛰️ EUMETSAT International Autumn School on Satellite Data Applications 2025  
Athens, Greece | 3-7 November 2025

Welcome to my workspace for the EUMETSAT Autumn School 2025 — a hands-on week exploring satellite data applications for weather, climate, and environmental monitoring.  
This repository is structured by training day and includes exercises, data, and scripts from each thematic block.

------------------------------------------------------------
📅 Agenda Overview

Day         | Focus                  | Main Topics
-------------|------------------------|----------------------------------------------
Monday       | EUMETView & Data Access | Remote sensing fundamentals, RGB products, WEkEO platform
Tuesday      | MTG Lightning Imager    | LI data access, visualization, parallax correction
Wednesday    | HSAF Products           | Precipitation, rain rate visualization, ADAGUC server
Thursday     | LSA SAF & Fire Products | LST, Fire detection and monitoring, LSASAF visualization
Friday       | DUST Monitoring         | LSA-SAF dust data, visualization, discussion & wrap-up

🌍 About the School

The EUMETSAT International Autumn School brings together students and professionals to apply satellite remote sensing to real-world problems in meteorology, hydrology, and climate.  
It combines theory with hands-on work using EUMETView, ADAGUC, LSASAF, and HSAF products.
